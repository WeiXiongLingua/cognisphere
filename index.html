What we refuse to put on a client's website

  • No stock photography of handshakes or glass towers If a firm lacks photography, we build the page around architectural diagrams, primary documents, and strong typography.
  • No decorative motion that slows reading Pages open immediately. Nothing hijacks the scroll wheel or forces a visitor to wait for paragraphs to fade in.
  • No fragile plugin stacks Your team should never have to worry that a routine plugin update will break the layout before an important meeting.

Practical questions

Straight answers on authorship, timelines, content editing, and post-launch ownership.

How do you make sure our website does not look like a template?

We begin every commission with your firm's actual subject matter—your buildings, clinical publications, transaction record, or industrial capabilities—and draw the layout from scratch in Figma before writing a single line of code. We do not use pre-made themes or component kits.

Who writes the text on the site?

We do, in close collaboration with you. Most projects stall at traditional agencies because the client is asked to supply all the copy in a shared folder. Instead, we interview your partners and draft clear, factual copy as part of the first milestone.

How is a two-week build possible without cutting corners?

We take on only two commissions a month and eliminate the bureaucratic overhead of large agencies. You work directly with the principal designer and engineer, decisions happen in real time on the Figma canvas, and our Next.js production architecture is streamlined for corporate sites.

Can our internal team update text and photographs after launch?

Yes. Every site includes a clean visual content management studio. Your team can edit headlines, body copy, case studies, team biographies, and images without touching code or risking layout shifts.

What happens after the site goes live?

Your firm owns the repository, domain configuration, and design files outright. There is no mandatory monthly retainer, and because the site is pre-rendered without a database of third-party plugins, ongoing hosting and maintenance remain minimal.
