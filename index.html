FAQs

Common questions

How long does a website take, and what do we need to provide?

Standard projects take exactly one month (four weeks). We run a structured 4-phase process: Week 1 for team discovery and bespoke copywriting, Week 2 for custom Figma design, Week 3 for custom engineering and visual editor setup, and Week 4 for cross-device QA, security checks, and live domain deployment.

Do we need to write our own website copy?

No. We interview your team to understand your products and target clients, then write clear, persuasive copy for every page. You review and approve the text before visual design begins.

Can our team easily edit text, news, and photos ourselves?

Yes. We configure an easy visual content editor. Anyone on your team can edit headlines, publish company news, update team bios, or swap images in minutes without touching any code.

How do hosting, security, and technical maintenance work?

We manage the codebase, high-speed cloud hosting, SSL certificates, daily backups, and security patches under a paid monthly or annual care plan, ensuring your website stays fast and trouble-free.

Will the website be mobile-optimized and ready for Google (SEO)?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first and fully responsive across phones, tablets, and desktops. We include fast loading speeds, clean semantic markup, and standard on-page SEO meta tags so search engines index your business properly.

Can we add more pages or additional languages later?

Yes. The architecture is modular and built to scale. As your business grows, adding new service pages, case studies, or extra languages (like German, French, or Spanish) can be done seamlessly anytime through our care plan.
