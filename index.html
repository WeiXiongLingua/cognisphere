// CANDID ANSWERS

Everything you want to know.

Will my website look like an AI-generated template?

Never. That is the exact problem we solve. AI landing pages all suffer from the same clichés: dark glossy neon gradients, generic icons, and vacuous buzzwords. Because our lead designer creates your visual identity from scratch in Figma before any code is generated, your website is 100% unique to your brand.

What do the humans do versus what the AI does?

The human team handles creative vision, brand strategy, visual aesthetics, bespoke Figma design, copywriting tone, and final device testing. The AI agents handle boilerplate code generation, responsive CSS compilation, Lighthouse performance optimizations, and Schema markup.

How fast can you really launch?

Most projects launch in 7 to 14 business days from kickoff. Traditional agencies take 3 to 5 months because they waste weeks on manual code assembly and internal bureaucracy. By augmenting our workflow with AI, we bypass the slog without cutting creative corners.

Can I update the text and photos myself after launch?

Yes. We connect a clean, intuitive visual CMS (Content Management System) where you and your team can edit copy, add case studies, and upload new photography with zero coding knowledge.

Are there any ongoing plugin or security headaches?

None. We do not use WordPress or fragile third-party plugins. Your site is compiled into static, pre-rendered Next.js edge assets with no vulnerable database or outdated plugins to patch.
