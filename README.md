# CogniSphere LLC — Official Homepage

Bespoke official websites crafted with human taste, engineered at AI velocity.

---

## 🎯 Positioning & Philosophy

- **Company Name:** CogniSphere LLC
- **Offering:** Bespoke official websites for businesses that refuse to look generic.
- **Core Value:** **Built by Human & AI together.**
  - **The Human Role:** Creative direction, emotional resonance, bespoke Figma UI/UX, typography hierarchy, brand narrative, and real-device quality signoff.
  - **The AI Role:** Instant Next.js edge code generation, Lighthouse 100 performance tuning, Schema.org SEO graph generation, and zero-bloat asset optimization.
- **Aesthetic:** Warm Editorial Craft meets Machine Precision (`#FAF9F5` warm paper canvas, `#FFFFFF` crisp cards, `#161513` deep ink text, `#C85A32` terracotta accent, `Newsreader` serif for editorial prestige, `Caveat` for authentic human handwritten annotations, and restrained `JetBrains Mono` for engineering metrics).

   - 3-way transparent breakdown: **Pure AI Generators** (generic, fragile) vs. **Legacy Agencies** (slow, bloated, $30k+) vs. **CogniSphere Human × AI** (bespoke, fast, 7–14 days).

   - Nordic Form Studio (Architecture)

8. **Human Inquiry Form:**
